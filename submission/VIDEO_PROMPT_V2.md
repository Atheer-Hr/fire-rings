## 1 · MASTER PROMPT (paste this)

Create a **30-second cinematic trailer** in the style of a NASA science documentary: dark, epic, precise, hopeful.
Hard limit 30.0 seconds, 16:9, 1080p, 24 fps. Burned-in subtitles on every spoken line.

**The story in one line:** For 25 years NASA satellites recorded every fire on Earth, but the first satellite saw blurry and the
second saw sharp — so the record falsely shows fire exploding. *Fire Calendar* corrects the record and tells anyone, in
seconds, whether this fire season is normal and when to get ready.

**Look and feel**
- Deep space blacks, Earth at night, ember and gold light. Palette: near-black #0E0B0A, ember #F97316, crimson #B91C1C,
  gold #FDE68A. Subtle film grain, anamorphic lens flares, slow camera moves.
- Typography: clean, bold sans-serif (like Readex Pro / Inter), white text, one key word highlighted in an ember-to-gold gradient.
- Motion design: fast, confident cuts in Act 1; calm, satisfying transitions in Act 2.
- Sound: deep low drone → satellite telemetry beeps → a "glitch" hit at 0:11 → rising orchestral swell at 0:14 → resolve at 0:27.
  Royalty-free music only.
- Do NOT draw NASA logos or insignia, do NOT invent numbers, do NOT redraw the website — use the attached screen recordings.

**Allowed numbers (all real):** 25 years · 565 million fire detections · raw record wrong by +718% · after correction 2.2% ·
forecast 40% better than the best simple method.
