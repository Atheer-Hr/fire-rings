## إذا اخترتم الشرائح بدل الفيديو (7 شرائح كحد أقصى، بالإنجليزي)

1. **Fire Rings** — From firefighting to fire-readiness · Team ASR Space, Madinah
2. **The problem:** 25 years of fire records, two sensors that can't be compared. Raw, the record overstates fire by 718%. MODIS is retiring.
3. **The bridge:** clean flares → fix Terra/Aqua and multi-VIIRS → physical (4 MW) + statistical bridges → blind test 2022–2025: bias 2%, R² 0.95.
4. **Fire Rings:** screenshot of the rings + calendar — one ring per year, one slice per month.
5. **Fire Forecast:** screenshot of the weather-style card · ML outlook, 41% lower error than the best simple method, tested blind on 2025.
6. **What we found:** Kalimantan Oct 2026 = 13× usual, season 10 days earlier · Asir: raw record +504% vs harmonized −16%.
7. **Impact & link:** civil defence, Saudi Green Initiative, health authorities · atheer-hr.github.io/fire-rings
